- **Ground Stop (GS) and Ground Delay Program (GDP) Protocols:** A GS requires aircraft meeting specific criteria (e.g., destination airport) to remain on the ground, often with little warning; ATC must not release aircraft during a GS without originator approval. A GDP holds aircraft on the ground to limit airborne holding and equitably assign delays system-wide. (`faa_7110_65.pdf`)
